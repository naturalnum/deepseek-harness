# Mode Gates

## Science

Split independent hypotheses, experiments, evidence review, and reproducibility checks. The Lead reviews claim boundaries and failed tests before one report publication.

## Medical Evidence

Keep retrieval, appraisal, applicability, safety, and synthesis attributable. Every material recommendation must resolve to registered evidence. The Lead performs the final safety and applicability review.

## Target Discovery

Workers may explore genetics, causal support, omics dependency, tractability, safety, or validation. They never call `target_portfolio(action="submit")`. The Lead first integrates exact commits, checks target identity, disease context, evidence ids, contrary findings, and validation gaps, then performs a second confirmation before the single canonical submit call.

## Financial Factor

Workers may propose mechanisms, audit point-in-time leakage, review implementation, or falsify results. They never call `factor_search`, `factor_registry`, checkpoint, or publish. The Lead owns one canonical search job, independently reviews the strongest candidates, verifies evidence and falsification, performs a second confirmation, then registers accepted factors once.

## Mathematics

Parallelize independent proof routes, formalization, counterexample search, and verification. The Lead reconciles assumptions and does not claim verification stronger than the available kernel or proof evidence.

## Challenge

Use the Challenge coordinator and its sealed task, evaluator, budget, and frontier contracts. Keep task acquisition and the one bounded SoTA retrieval route Lead-owned and serial. Team controls parallel candidate reasoning; the Python coordinator controls experiment parallelism and assigns exclusive GPU device leases from declared `gpu_slots`. Workers never choose GPU IDs. Only the Lead may promote or submit a candidate.

## General Projects

Use independent implementation, review, evidence, and falsification lanes. The Lead owns integration, final verification, and publication.
