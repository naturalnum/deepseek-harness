# XAlpha Orchestration

## Report-to-Memory Absorption

Preserve source identity, remove presentation noise, and retain mechanism-bearing text. Filter material that cannot be tested under the frozen data contract. Extract broad mechanism families, then route each cluster into concrete research archetypes. These records are hypotheses and research cues, not validation evidence.

## Macro Brain

Choose one routing mode: fixed theme, coarse-guided, or memory-driven. Select one primary mechanism, supporting mechanisms, a bounded active archetype set, and falsifiable hypothesis targets. Macro work is strategic: do not write formulas, code, windows, or performance claims.

## Micro Brain

Generate one factor per candidate record. Keep hypothesis, explanation, implementation, and financial plausibility aligned. Evolve mechanisms rather than only changing syntax:

- Mutation changes one meaningful mechanism axis.
- Crossover preserves an indispensable mechanism from each parent without naive additive blending.
- Refinement improves implementation or robustness while preserving the hypothesis.
- Novelty introduces a genuinely new path outside the current local family.
- Repair makes the smallest change required by a concrete failed gate.

## Cross Brain

Classify the implemented factor from its actual final mechanism. GOOD feedback records positive evidence, a reusable principle, and what must not be copied mechanically. BAD feedback records the failure type, failed assumption, avoidance rule, and repair condition. Keep feedback bounded and deduplicated.
