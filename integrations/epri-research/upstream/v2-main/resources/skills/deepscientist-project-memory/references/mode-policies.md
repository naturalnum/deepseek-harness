# Mode policies

Read only the section for the active mode. These are deposition filters, not extra mandatory tasks.

## General and science

- Methods: reusable data checks, experiment controls, reproduction steps, and decision rules.
- Knowledge: verified dataset properties, environment constraints, and scoped conclusions.
- Lessons: failed assumptions, non-reproducible conditions, and applicability limits.

## Mathematical research

- Preserve verified lemmas, reusable proof reductions, counterexamples, formalization constraints, and failed routes with a clear obstruction.
- Point to proof files, Lean artifacts, calculations, or review evidence. Do not store speculative proof sketches as confirmed knowledge.

## Financial research and factor discovery

- Preserve data semantics, as-of rules, universe and cost assumptions, reproducible factor definitions, regime limits, and falsified mechanisms.
- Point to code, manifests, backtests, robustness outputs, or review files. Never promote a factor from an in-sample metric alone.

## Target discovery and medical evidence

- Preserve cohort definitions, assay or database versions, evidence boundaries, population applicability, negative findings, and safety constraints.
- Use aggregate or permitted evidence pointers. Never store patient identifiers or unsupported clinical recommendations.

## Long-running goals

- Preserve only findings that change the frontier: a supported/refuted hypothesis, exhausted route, reusable method, or verified blocker.
- Transient progress, retry logs, and unchanged status do not belong in memory.

## Team Mode

- Workers identify candidate entries with source pointers alongside their primary result when something durable was learned; they do not promote shared memory.
- After integrating Worker commits, the Lead must perform one reconciliation before the final checkpoint or publication and promotes at most the few entries that remain useful after deduplication and contradiction review.
