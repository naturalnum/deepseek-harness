# Chinese Official-Document Routing

Use this reference only after `ds-document` has identified the reader,
authority and requested action. It is a routing aid, not a substitute for the
issuing institution's current template, policy manual or approval process.

## Document routes

| User intent | Prefer | Required center of gravity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| State verified progress or findings to a superior | 报告 / 专报 / 汇报材料 | facts, basis, problems, recommendation or requested instruction |
| Ask a superior for instruction, approval or resources | 请示 | one clear request, reason, options, proposed action and expected support |
| Deploy a rule, task or arrangement to a defined audience | 通知 | scope, effective time, required action, owner and contact |
| Inform the public or a broad audience of an official matter | 公告 / 通告 | issuing authority, object, factual content, effective date and compliance wording |
| Record an official decision or disposition | 决定 / 决议 / 批复 | decision text, basis, scope, effective date and responsible implementation |
| Communicate between units without an upward/downward command relation | 函 | purpose, facts, request or response, recipient and contact |
| Record a meeting | 纪要 | attendees, agenda, decisions, owners, deadlines and unresolved items |
| Organize future work | 工作方案 / 工作计划 | objective, scope, phases, responsibility, schedule, resources, risks and acceptance |
| Explain a study or investigation | 调研报告 | question, method/source, findings, limitations, options and recommendations |
| Record completed work | 工作总结 | objective, actions, results, gaps, lessons and next actions |
| Deliver spoken remarks | 讲话稿 | occasion, audience, message hierarchy, action and closing |

Use a more formal legal/administrative document type only when the user gives
the required authority and issuance context. Otherwise label the output as a
draft, memo, briefing or proposed text.

## Reader and direction

Before writing, classify the relation:

- **向上**: report or request; do not phrase a recommendation as an order.
- **向下**: notice or arrangement; state the responsible unit and required
  action without vague collective verbs.
- **平行/协作**: letter or coordination memo; state what the recipient needs
  to do and by when.
- **对外**: public-facing notice; verify authority, scope, privacy and release
  status before using definitive language.

If the relationship is unclear, use neutral wording and ask the owner to select
the route. A document's politeness must not hide who decides or who acts.

## Minimal structure

Most official drafts can be reduced to these blocks:

1. **标题/主题**: identify the action or subject, not a marketing slogan.
2. **背景与目的**: why this document exists now.
3. **事实与依据**: sourced facts, policy basis, date/version and scope.
4. **事项/分析**: what happened, what is proposed, and what remains uncertain.
5. **请求/要求/决定**: one clearly scannable action block.
6. **责任与时限**: owner, dependency, deadline and contact.
7. **附件/分送/备注**: only when real and authorized.

For a `请示`, keep the requested decision singular whenever possible. For a
`纪要`, do not turn discussion opinions into resolutions. For a `通知`, keep
background short and make the operative requirements explicit.

## Language guardrails

- Do not manufacture发文字号、签发日期、印章、主送机关、抄送机关、政策条款
  or statistics. Use placeholders when absent.
- Do not turn “建议、拟、可、待定” into “必须、应当、已经决定”.
- Keep the same name for a unit, person, project and metric throughout.
- Define abbreviations at first use and preserve original quoted terms.
- Separate “现状事实”, “判断”, “方案选项” and “建议” with headings or labels.
- Avoid absolute claims such as “全面、彻底、唯一、零风险” unless an
  authoritative source and scope justify them.
